Plain-language summary of a carrier and broker's published equipment guide. Actual payload depends on the tractor and trailer's empty weight under the 80,000-pound gross limit.

## Dry vans

Maximum freight weight: 44,000 pounds for a 53-foot high cube dry van, 45,000 pounds for a 48-foot high cube dry van.

## Refrigerated trailers

Maximum freight weight: 43,500 pounds for a 53-foot reefer, 44,000 pounds for a 48-foot reefer.
