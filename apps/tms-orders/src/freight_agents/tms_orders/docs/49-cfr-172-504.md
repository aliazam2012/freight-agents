## Table 1: placarded in any quantity

A vehicle carrying any amount of these must be placarded: divisions 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 explosives, 2.3 poison gas, 4.3 dangerous when wet, 5.2 organic peroxide type B temperature controlled, 6.1 materials poisonous by inhalation, and class 7 with a Radioactive Yellow III label.

## Table 2: placarded from 1,001 pounds

Divisions 1.4, 1.5 and 1.6, 2.1 flammable gas, 2.2 non-flammable gas, class 3 flammable, combustible liquid, 4.1 flammable solid, 4.2 spontaneously combustible, 5.1 oxidizer, 5.2 organic peroxide other than type B temperature controlled, 6.1 poison other than inhalation hazard, 6.2 (no placard), class 8 corrosive and class 9.

## 172.504(c) The 1,001-pound exception

By highway or rail, a transport vehicle or freight container carrying less than 454 kg (1,001 pounds) aggregate gross weight of Table 2 materials needs no placard. The exception does not apply to bulk packagings or to materials covered by 172.505, and never to Table 1 materials.
