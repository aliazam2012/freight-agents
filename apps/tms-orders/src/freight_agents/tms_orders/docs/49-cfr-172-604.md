## 172.604(a) The number

Whoever offers a hazardous material for transportation must give a numeric emergency response telephone number with its area code. It must be monitored at all times the material is in transportation, by a person who knows the material and its emergency response, or who can reach such a person at once; an answering service or a call-back number does not qualify. It appears right after the shipping description, or once on the shipping paper as an emergency contact when it covers every hazardous material listed.
