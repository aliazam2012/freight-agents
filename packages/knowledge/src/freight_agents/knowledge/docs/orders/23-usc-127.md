## 127(a) Interstate weight limits

On the Interstate System the limits, including enforcement tolerances, are 20,000 pounds on a single axle, 34,000 pounds on a tandem axle, and an overall gross weight of 80,000 pounds, with the bridge formula limiting the weight on any group of consecutive axles. Exceptions exist for specific routes and permitted non-divisible loads.
