## 172.202(a) Required elements

A hazardous material's shipping description names its identification number (such as UN1263), its proper shipping name, its hazard class or division, and its packing group in Roman numerals where the material has one (class 1, division 5.2 and self-reactive materials have none), with the total quantity and its unit.

## 172.202(b) Sequence

The identification number, proper shipping name, hazard class and packing group appear in that order with nothing in between, for example "UN2744, Cyclobutyl chloroformate, 6.1, (8, 3), PG II".
