## 14706(a) Carrier liability

A motor carrier under the Surface Transportation Board's jurisdiction must issue a receipt or bill of lading for property it accepts for transportation. The carrier is liable to the person entitled to recover for the actual loss or injury to the property it causes.

## 14706(e) Minimum period to file a claim

A carrier may not, by rule, contract or otherwise, give a claimant less than 9 months to file a claim against it, or less than 2 years to bring a civil action. The 2 years for a lawsuit run from the date the carrier gives written notice that it has disallowed any part of the claim. The 9 months is a floor: a bill of lading may allow longer, never shorter. Bills of lading commonly measure the period from the date of delivery, or from a reasonable time for delivery when the goods never arrive.
